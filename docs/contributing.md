# Contributing

As Gravinium continues to grow, extra help becomes a necessary component to our existence.
We believe that everyone has a unique experience, prospective, and skill
set that we as Gravinium can benefit greatly from.

Contributing to Gravinium can come in many forms, too many to fully list. A small sample would be:

1. Helping manage group instances
2. Creating material used in or by Gravinium
3. Suggesting new projects/events/etc
4. Voicing opinions over past decisions

## Where to get started

If you find what we do enjoyable, we'd love to have you around. The best place to start
would be our [discord server](https://gravinium.org/discord). There,
you can talk with us and learn a bit about how we do things. If you feel that anything you
do fits well, or think we can try something new that you can bring to the table, do voice
it through the discord!

Here are some common places to start!

- <strong>_Do you program?_</strong> Check out our [GitHub](https://github.com/Gravinium-VR) and maybe
  submit some code changes.
- <strong>_Do you make art?_</strong> We are always looking for help on posters, assets, and a bunch more!
  The [discord](https://gravinium.org/discord) is a great place to start with that.
- <strong>_Do you like writing?_</strong> We can always use help on these docs! Check out the [repo](https://github.com/Gravinium-VR/gravinium-docs)
  for more information on how to contribute.
- <strong>_Do you DJ/produce?_</strong> Our rave group, [Module](https://vrlink.me/module) is in need of new
  people to play at. Message us in the [discord](https://gravinium.org/discord) to reserve
  a slot.
- <strong>_None of those fit?_</strong> No worries, we can't list everything! Please get in touch with us
  so we can figure out the best place for you. I'm certain a spot exists.

# General Guidelines

We won't lay out any hard guidelines, however Gravinium maintains an inclusive environment,
and we take seriously if our members are harassed in any way. We observe the general ideas
of PLUR across our community.

<strong>Peace</strong>, <strong>Love</strong>, <strong>Unity</strong>, and <strong>Respect</strong>.

Help us maintain this environment.
